A family kitchen with a market next to the grill

Wally's is a family-owned Lebanese spot on Cesery Blvd. The meats are halal, and the menu covers the classics: gyro and shawarma wraps, kafta, falafel, kibbeh, and platters loaded with grape leaves, hummus and tabouleh.

Pick up Middle Eastern groceries while you wait for your order.
